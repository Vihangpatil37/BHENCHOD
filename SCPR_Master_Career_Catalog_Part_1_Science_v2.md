# Science Stream Overview

Science is ideal for students interested in Engineering, Technology,
Medicine, Research, Defence, Aviation, Mathematics and Innovation.

**Core Streams** - PCM (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics) - PCB (Physics,
Chemistry, Biology) - PCMB (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology)
